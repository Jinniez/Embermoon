# EMBERMOON

EMBERMOON is a dreamy, macaron-style metaphysics site — Eastern, Western, and Vedic, in one place.

## Why EMBERMOON

Western astrology apps know the stars, but they don't know Chinese metaphysics.

Bazi, Ziwei, Liuyao, and Bagua have been practiced for thousands of years, yet almost no one outside China can use them — no English, no Spanish, no easy way in.

EMBERMOON exists to change that. We bring Eastern, Western, and Vedic systems together in one place, in three languages, so anyone can look up at the same sky, in their own way.

## What you can do here

- **Eastern** — Bazi, Ziwei Dou Shu, Liuyao, Bagua
- **Western** — Tarot, Natal Chart, Synastry, Transits, Solar Return
- **Vedic** — Kundli, Nakshatra, Dasha, Remedies, Navamsa
- **Learn** — short guides to each system, one article at a time

## Languages

Chinese / English / Spanish — switch anytime, your chart stays as it is.

1. Pick a system and enter your birth info.
2. Get your chart — no account needed.
3. Copy the result and take it anywhere for a reading.
