# EMBERMOON

Website: https://jinniez.github.io/Embermoon/

EMBERMOON is a dreamy, macaron-style metaphysics site — Chinese, Western, and Vedic, in one place.

## Why EMBERMOON

Western astrology apps know the stars, but they don't know Chinese metaphysics.

Bazi, Ziwei, Liuyao, and Meihua Yishu have been practiced for thousands of years, yet almost no one outside China can use them — no English, no Spanish, no easy way in.

EMBERMOON exists to change that. We bring Chinese, Western, and Vedic systems together in one place, in three languages, so anyone can look up at the same sky, in their own way.

## What you can do here

- **Chinese** — Bazi (with AI chat reading), Ziwei Dou Shu, Liuyao, Meihua Yishu
- **Western** — Tarot, Natal Chart, Synastry, Transits, Solar Return
- **Vedic** — Kundli, Nakshatra, Dasha, Remedies, Navamsa
- **Learn** — short guides to each system, one article at a time

## Bazi AI chat
